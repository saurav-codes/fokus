fokus is a free, passwordless co-focus timer. Create a focus room, share the
link, and anyone with a display name can join the same countdown. Choose from
Camel, Pomodoro, or custom cycle sequences. Other participants always see the
same timer, and every person keeps their own focus stats.

No signup, no account, no ads. Open source (MIT) and built with Capacitor
around the hosted https://fokus.lazyplanner.app UI.

Source: https://github.com/saurav-codes/fokus
